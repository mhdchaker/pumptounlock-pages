Questions

Do I need an account?

No. PumpToUnlock works without an account, and we never ask for your name or email.

Does the camera record me?

The camera picture that counts your push-ups is processed on your iPhone and is never uploaded.

Which apps can I lock?

Any app or category on your iPhone. You pick them from Apple’s Screen Time list, and everything else stays open.

How many push-ups does a minute cost?

You decide. The setting is called Intensity.

How do I cancel Elite?

In the App Store, under your name, then Subscriptions. Elite stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there an Android version?

No. PumpToUnlock is for iPhone only.
